Common Questions

Answers for the board.

Is this legal advice or a licence?

No. TrustBlocks provides an independent, deterministic assessment of your controls and their evidence. It complements — and never replaces — qualified legal counsel or a supervisory authorisation.

What does “deterministic” mean here?

The same inputs always produce the same result. No generative AI writes your findings, so two people running the same assessment obtain an identical, reproducible outcome — and an identical cryptographic seal.

How is the evidence verifiable by a third party?

Each report is sealed with a SHA-256 cryptographic proof. A supervisor or counterparty can re-compute the fingerprint and confirm the report has not been altered since it was issued.

Which frameworks do you cover?

MiCA, DORA, VARA, SAMA, MAS and the EU AI Act — mapped so a single assessment can answer multiple supervisors at once.
