How we handle your professional data

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This notice explains how TrustBlocks processes personal data of business contacts: prospects, clients, partners and people who write to us. It is provided under Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

The controller is TrustBlocks, represented by Nirinasoa Rajaona, Paris, France. For any question or request about your data, write to rajaona@trustaria.io.

2. What data we process

  • Identity and role: name, job title, company.
  • Professional contact details: business email, LinkedIn profile, business phone number if you gave it to us.
  • Relationship data: our exchanges with you, meeting notes, follow-up dates and the stage of our discussions.

We do not seek or process sensitive data (health, political opinions, etc.). Please do not send us such data.

3. Where the data comes from

Directly from you (messages, emails, meetings, business cards), or from public professional sources: LinkedIn, your company's website, public registers and professional events.

4. Why we use it and on what legal basis

PurposeLegal basis
Contacting professionals about services relevant to their role (B2B prospecting) and following up our discussionsOur legitimate interest in developing our business (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
Preparing and performing a contract or a quote you asked forPre-contractual steps and contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b))
Keeping accounting and legal recordsLegal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

Our messages are sent to your professional address and relate to your professional role. You can object at any time, without giving a reason, by replying "stop" or writing to the address above.

5. Who receives the data

Your data is not sold or rented. It is accessed only by TrustBlocks and by service providers acting on our instructions, limited to what they need:

  • customer-relationship (CRM) and task-tracking tools, used to record contacts and follow-up dates;
  • email and office tools;
  • website hosting;
  • AI writing assistants, only where we use them, and with the minimum data needed (for example a name, a company and a follow-up date, never the full content of confidential exchanges).

Client reports, on-chain findings and analysed information remain confidential and are covered by our engagement terms, not by this prospecting process.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some providers are established in, or access data from, the United States or other countries outside the EEA. In that case, transfers rely on an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified providers) or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for details.

7. How long we keep it

  • Prospects: up to 3 years after our last contact with you, then deleted.
  • Clients: for the duration of the relationship, then for the periods required by law (for example 10 years for accounting documents).
  • If you object, we keep only what is needed to stop contacting you.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its use, receive a copy (portability where applicable), and object to its use, including to prospecting at any time. Write to rajaona@trustaria.io. We answer within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country.

9. Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows the latest version.
